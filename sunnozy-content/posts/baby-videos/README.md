## Stil

**Strecken** (Standard): nach dem viralen Reel von @zayac_mehovoi (2.8 Mio. Views): Handy in der Hand, Kamera fast still, Baby verschlafen, streckt langsam die Arme und endet in der «Hände hoch»-Pose, kleines Lächeln.

```
Vertical handheld smartphone video filmed by a mum standing next to the bed, the photo is the first frame. Real, unedited phone footage: the camera keeps the angle of the photo and stays almost still, only a soft natural hand sway and slow tiny reframing like a human holding a phone. No zoom, no cinematic camera moves, no gimbal. Soft daylight, bright airy colours, slight phone camera noise. The newborn is sleepy and drowsy, eyes closed or only half open, and does a slow, soft newborn stretch like real newborns do: one arm slowly rises up next to the head with a little fist, then the other arm stretches out to the side, and both arms end up above the head in a relaxed hands-up pose. Small, slightly jerky newborn movements, a little sleepy smile and tiny mouth movements, the legs bend and stretch slowly once, the head turns only a little. Then the baby settles and stays sleepy. Calm, soft breathing, barely visible, no heavy breathing. No crying, no fully waking up, no fast movements, no rolling over. Keep the same baby, face, clothing, prints, colours, bedding and room exactly as in the photo. No new objects, no text, no slow motion, no filters. Sound: quiet room ambience, no music.
```

**Still** (erste Runde): Baby schläft durch und bewegt sich kaum, Bauch atmet ganz flach.

```
Handheld smartphone video filmed by a mum standing next to the baby, the photo is the first frame. Real, unedited phone footage held in a person's hand: the camera moves extremely slowly and only very little, a soft natural sway and tiny hand tremor like a human holding a phone, at most a barely noticeable slow drift closer. No zoom, no fast or big camera moves, no cinematic camera moves, no gimbal, no dolly, no orbit. Natural window light, soft phone camera noise, the phone autofocus adjusting once. The baby keeps sleeping peacefully the whole time, eyes stay closed, and lies almost completely still. Breathing is very soft, slow and shallow, like a real sleeping newborn: the tummy rises and falls only very slightly and slowly, barely visible. At most one tiny finger twitch. No heavy or fast breathing, no waking up, no opening eyes, no yawning, no stretching, no arm or leg movements, no head turning. If the baby is awake in the photo, it stays calm and almost completely still and only blinks slowly. Keep the same baby, face, clothing, prints, colours, blanket and room exactly as in the photo. No new objects, no text, no slow motion, no filters. Sound: quiet room ambience, no music.
```

## Produkt-Schwenk (24.11.2026)

`<foto>_schwenk.mp4`: pro Set ein Video, Kamerafahrt nach dem Muster aus der Bildschirmaufnahme vom 24.11. Grok Imagine, Video-Modus, 720p, 10 s, Foto als erstes Bild. Ausgangsfotos aus `baby-photos/referenzen_photos_neu/`, bei Sets mit Ads-Variante die `*_ads.png`.

Ablauf: Handy geht nah ans Gesicht (0–2 s), gleitet am Outfit runter bis zu den Füssen (2–5 s), bleibt kurz bei Beinen und Füssen (5–7 s) und fährt zurück zum Gesicht (7–10 s). Ende ≈ Anfang, damit das Reel loopt.

Prompt «Produkt-Schwenk»:

> Vertical handheld smartphone video, the photo is the first frame. Real, unedited phone footage: a mum holds her phone close above her baby and slowly films the outfit up close. 0-2 s: the phone moves in close until the baby fills most of the frame, framed on the face and the hat or head. 2-5 s: the phone slowly glides down along the body, close-up over the chest, the print, the buttons and the fabric texture, down to the legs and the little feet. 5-7 s: it drifts a little to the side and lingers close on the legs and feet. 7-10 s: it slowly glides back up along the outfit and ends close on the face again, like a seamless loop. Close-up the whole time, the camera follows the outfit, slightly tilted like a real phone, soft natural hand sway, smooth and slow, no zoom jumps, no cuts, no gimbal or drone moves, nothing fast. The outfit stays sharp: fabric, print, colours and details exactly as in the photo. The baby stays calm and lies still on the back: if the eyes are closed the baby keeps sleeping peacefully, if awake the baby only blinks softly. Barely visible calm breathing, at most one tiny hand movement. No crying, no rolling over. Same baby, face, clothing, bedding and room as in the photo. Do not add anything: no toys, no blanket, no pacifier, no text. Soft daylight, warm natural colours, slight phone camera noise. Sound: quiet room ambience, no music.
